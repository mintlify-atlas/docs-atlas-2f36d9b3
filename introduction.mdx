## What is Kairo?

Kairo is an intelligent learning ecosystem built for Riwi's intensive bootcamp programs. It bridges the gap between a fast-paced curriculum and individual learning needs by analyzing academic performance and soft skills to generate personalized study plans — so no Coder falls behind.

The platform serves two distinct user roles:

- **Coders** — Bootcamp students who receive AI-generated learning plans, access an AI tutor, complete exercises, and submit evidence of their work.
- **Team Leaders (TLs)** — Instructors and mentors who monitor cohort performance, identify at-risk students, provide feedback, and generate AI reports.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Coders register, verify their email, and complete a soft skills self-assessment covering autonomy, time management, communication, teamwork, and problem solving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive a personalized plan">
    Kairo's AI engine (powered by Groq's LLM) analyzes the soft skills assessment and Moodle academic progress data to generate a custom 4-week study plan with prioritized focus cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through daily activities">
    Coders complete guided, semi-guided, and autonomous activities, submit evidence, and chat with the AI tutor for support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team Leaders monitor and intervene">
    TLs track completion rates, review evidence submissions, provide feedback, and use AI-generated reports to proactively identify at-risk Coders before academic failure occurs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Architecture

Kairo runs as a distributed system with three independent services:

| Service | Technology | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **API Gateway** | Node.js + Express | Business logic, auth, session management |
| **AI Microservice** | Python + FastAPI | LLM inference, plan generation, embeddings |
| **Database** | PostgreSQL (Supabase) | Persistent storage for users, plans, and logs |

See the [Architecture overview](/architecture) for the full system diagram and service interactions.
